Maiden Voyage · 14 April 1912

It is half past eleven on a moonless night, four days out of Southampton, and the sea is a flat calm. You have the ship. Six ice warnings have come in by wireless today; two of them never reached the bridge.

She is 269 metres long and ten decks deep, and you may walk every one of them — the Grand Staircase, the Dining Saloon, Scotland Road, the swimming bath, the mail room, and the six boiler rooms where 159 furnaces are being driven hard.

She answers the helm very slowly. That is not a bug — it is the whole of the problem.
